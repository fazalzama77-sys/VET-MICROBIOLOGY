Veterinary Microbiology Studio

VETERINARY CURRICULUM COMPANION
Overview

A comprehensive, interactive curriculum companion for B.V.Sc. & A.H. Second Year Veterinary Microbiology (VCI MSVE 2016 Syllabus). Designed for veterinary undergraduate students, it integrates 5 theory units, 5 practical units, systematic bacteriology, mycology, biotechnology, immunology, virology notes, clinical correlations, question banks, quizzes and an offline-first revision dashboard.

Vision & Mission
  • Complete VCI Syllabus: Full coverage of General & Systematic Bacteriology, Veterinary Mycology, Microbial Biotechnology, Immunology & Serology, and General & Systematic Virology.
  • Diagnostic Logic: Pair every pathogen with morphology, staining, culture characteristics, virulence determinants, and diagnostic protocols.
  • Active Recall: Unit-wise quizzes, paper-wise practice, spaced repetition review, and written exam Q&A banks.
  • Accessible & Offline: 100% free, fast, responsive PWA that runs offline on any phone or laptop.
